# 🌿 SchemeWise

> **Discover. Check. Understand. Verify.**

SchemeWise is a health-focused civic-tech web application designed to make government health-support information easier to discover and understand.

Instead of requiring users to already know the name of a government scheme, SchemeWise starts with the user's **need and situation** and guides them toward potentially relevant health schemes.

> ⚠️ **DISCLAIMER:** SchemeWise provides information and eligibility guidance only. Final eligibility and approval are determined by the respective scheme authority. Always verify current details on official government websites before applying.

# 🎯 PROLEM STATEMENT

Government health schemes provide important support, but users may face difficulties such as:

- Not knowing which scheme is relevant to their situation
- Searching through multiple government websites
- Understanding complex eligibility conditions
- Comparing different schemes
- Knowing why a particular scheme may be relevant
- Understanding what documents or information may be required
- Finding the appropriate official source
- Difficulty navigating large amounts of government information

The problem is therefore not simply a lack of information.

The problem is **information discovery, understanding and navigation**.
